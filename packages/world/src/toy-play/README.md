# Toy parts

The toy art direction's shared parts: `parts.tsx` (blocks, balls, discs, trees,
flowers, lighting), `style.ts` (the `TOY` palette), `geometry.ts` (rounded slabs
and rings), `label-layout.ts` (keeping DOM labels apart) and `assets.ts`. The
island games in `../game-kit` and the lesson stage are built from them
(ADR-0011).

The garden editions, the purpose-built factory, workshop and flight scenes and
their engines that once lived here were deleted on 2026-10-01 (Owner decision
G3). Their screenshots stay in
`docs/reference/interaction-components/album.html`.
