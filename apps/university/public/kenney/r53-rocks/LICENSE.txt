Selected originals: rock_largeA.glb, rock_largeD.glb, rock_largeF.glb,
plant_flatShort.glb, plant_bush.glb and mushroom_tan.glb. Original files are not modified. University bakes welded,
normalized vertex/triangle data, closes the D/F support solids with a convex
hull, and applies its own mineral/leaf palette. No donor texture is included.
R54 preserves mushroom stem/cap material roles and uses a quiet University
woodland palette. Leaf backs are emitted explicitly in the shared opaque batch.

Reproducible source hashes and per-file adaptations are recorded in
packages/world/src/island/kenney-rock-shapes.json. The offline Blender script is
apps/university/scripts/bake-island-rocks.py. Neither Blender nor the local donor
directory is a runtime dependency.
