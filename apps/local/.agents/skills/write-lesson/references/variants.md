# Five content-led lenses within PRIMM

The five names remain `现象`, `对比`, `溯源`, `决策`, `术语`. They describe the
learning problem, not a visual component or a quota. These are University content categories, not five variants claimed by PRIMM authors.
Each new pilot lesson keeps Predict → Run → Investigate → Modify → Make. The
category helps choose materials and operations, especially in Investigate and
Modify; it never reorders or omits phases to fit a component.

| Variant | Main action | Must preserve | Typical failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 现象 | Observe a real result, try or inspect a change, explain what happened | Actual conditions and causal limits; a short worked example if needed | Inventing surprise, or a flashy effect that teaches no mechanism |
| 对比 | Apply/compare two approaches to a common material and choose by purpose | Same and different cases, when each approach fits | A table of names without consequences |
| 溯源 | Trace a result back through real records/code/system steps | Evidence at each meaningful step, observed versus documented distinction | Inventing a path or confusing a reachable link with support |
| 决策 | Choose under clear constraints, inspect benefit/cost, change the constraints | A case where the earlier choice should be reversed or deferred | One universal best answer, all toggles on, no exploration phase |
| 术语 | Recognize a word in real uses and distinguish nearby meanings | Positive uses and what the term does not mean | Dictionary copying or sorting by superficial words |

Pick the dominant action that prepares independent application. Do not rotate a
misfitting shape merely for variety. Review a third consecutive same variant:
record why it is content-led rather than silently manufacturing a new variant.

## Responsibilities, not headings

Predict → Run → Investigate → Modify → Make. Everyday relevance and real-world
context belong at Predict entry; explanations sit where the task needs them. Show task-specific headings and concrete actions to the
learner, not the author's labels. Opening may be normal, not surprising.
A decision can be multiple choice; an answer can be a visible result; a summary
need not be another interaction. At least one meaningful action must require a
judgment or construction, not merely clicking to reveal all text.

For `决策`, preserve the substance of “什么时候该反过来”, visibly in the path or
its related explanation, not only buried in full prose. For `术语`, likewise
preserve “它不是什么”. PRIMM has no mandatory University heading strings for these duties.

## Stored legacy content

Ordinary non-path revisions still validate against the existing prose-spine
rules in `scripts/lesson-spine.mjs`: open section, one open prediction and its
invitation, answer, content-led middle, question-only self-check, one-line recap.
V1 path revisions retain their own earlier schema. Do not silently relax those
stored contracts, alter old revision bytes, or run V2 checks over untouched courses.
A new V2 revision declares `pedagogyVersion: 2` in its existing path activity.
