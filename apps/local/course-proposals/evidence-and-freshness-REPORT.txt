Most counter-intuitive finding (taught in-course)
-------------------------------------------------
Inference evidence can go stale even when the cited file's blob objectId is unchanged.

In evaluateEvidenceFreshness, if kind === "inference" and the target snapshot's sourceCommit has moved forward, the code still pushes
"Inference requires review after the repository commit changes" while sameRegularBlob is true.

That is the opposite of the naive rule "if the lines didn't change, the lesson is still fine."
A fact can stay fresh on an identical blob across commits; a judgment cannot — surrounding call graph and defaults may have shifted
while those exact lines stayed put. Marking uncertain claims as inference is what makes the curriculum re-open them.

Also sharp (lesson cascade-active-to-stale): apply only transitions active → stale; a later "fresh" report never auto-revives a course.
