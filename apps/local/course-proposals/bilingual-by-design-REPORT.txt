Most counter-intuitive finding (already in the course)
------------------------------------------------------
fallbackLng is 'zh-CN', not 'en'.

Western i18n scaffolds almost always anchor missing keys on English. TuringPact
does the opposite: en is a first-class resource tree, but the configured fallback
language is Chinese. That matches a product whose source copy and primary market
are Chinese — so an incomplete English key surfaces as Chinese text (or forces
you to notice mixed UI) rather than sprinkling English into a Chinese session by
default. Lesson: fallback-is-zh-cn (unit fallback-and-detection). The mermaid
diagram in that lesson highlights the B→D edge: miss current locale → look up
zh-CN, not “whatever else is loaded”.
