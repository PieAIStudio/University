## Most counterintuitive finding (taught in lesson ai-score-anti-skip)
pickAiScorePersona does not merely prefer AI_SCORE_KEYWORDS. It subtracts 10
whenever SKIP_KEYWORDS hit, and when bestScore <= 0 it forces the first non-skip
option. That is acceptance engineering: force the cooperation persona off the
skipper track so mianzi|ai_score transcriptDiffs stay > 0 (ADR-0007 + unit tests).
Without the -10 penalty, two personas collapse on skip-heavy questionnaire nodes
and the product axes stop being regression-visible.
