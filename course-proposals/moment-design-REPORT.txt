Most counter-intuitive finding (taught in lesson silent-placeholder-playing)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
AMBIENCE_LOOP_URL is deliberately null (asset gap under a ~300KB loop budget),
yet the opening ritual still reaches audioPhase "playing". ambienceAudio starts a
zero-gain OscillatorNode so the Web Audio graph and product state machine complete
without a real tavern loop. The memorable "moment" is gated on state completion,
not on media assets being ready — without the silent placeholder, unlock would
stall and e2e data-attributes would never observe playing.
