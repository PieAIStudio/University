Most counterintuitive finding (already in the course)
-----------------------------------------------------
clueDiversityScorer does NOT read publicMessage. Its entire score is:

  styleSignal === annotation.expectedClueSignal ? 1 : 0

So a dimension named like “style/clue quality” is actually a fixture
metadata consistency check. You can ship robotic corporate copy and still
get clueDiversity = 1 if the two metadata strings match. Real pressure on
tone comes from humanChatStyle, quality violations (e.g.
public_message_too_long), and live model output — not from this scorer id.

Closely related (also taught): offline runM1EvalCase always scores
output: input.output (the gold/poison written in the JSON). Offline green
means the ruler + labels still agree; only live (runMastraAgentDecision)
measures the current model — and fails if totalCostUsd >= 0.02.
