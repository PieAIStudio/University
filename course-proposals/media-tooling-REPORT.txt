Most counter-intuitive finding (also taught in-course)
------------------------------------------------------
portrait-matte does not treat "green distance" as the only alpha signal.
Pixels with low greenDominance (G - max(R,B) ≤ 12) are force-opaque even when
they sit inside the soft key-ray band. Without foregroundGreenDominanceCeiling,
hair and skin would go semi-transparent under pure distance matting—the failure
mode that shows up only after batching dozens of plates.

A second sharp edge on the voice side: --sync refuses scoped chapter/language
runs and requires a reviewed planDigest + expected-missing before any MiniMax
call. Partial paid writes are treated as more dangerous than a hard stop.
