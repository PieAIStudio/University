# Measure world-island dominance and fit together

## Guidance

When tuning a procedural world catalogue, validate island-group screen occupancy on both axes together with an explicit no-cropping boundary check; a close camera can fake dominance by cutting off the field. Derive island footprint from the real course-length distribution and the generated outline's measured unit extent, rather than from cell count alone, so the shortest real courses remain clickable while long outliers stay legible without merging neighbours. Apply this whenever world composition or course-length scaling changes.

## Applies When

- The work is complete and verified.
- The lesson is non-obvious, reusable, and not already documented.
