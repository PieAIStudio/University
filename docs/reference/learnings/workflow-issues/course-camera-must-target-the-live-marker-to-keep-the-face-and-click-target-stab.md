# Course camera must target the live marker to keep the face and click target stable

## Guidance

When tightening a course-island camera, anchor both camera and look target to the current live lesson, keep the eye on the avatar's front side, and verify a serpentine mid-path state in a real browser. A target aimed at a later lesson can swing the camera with the road, turn the avatar off-center, show the back of its head, and leave the live DOM control under another overlay; a local target just behind and above the live marker keeps the face, the live action, and a short readable run of the path stable while pan/zoom remain available.

## Applies When

- The work is complete and verified.
- The lesson is non-obvious, reusable, and not already documented.
